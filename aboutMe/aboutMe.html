about me

Hi! My name is Agustin Abdala and I am photographer, amateur designer and film student with knowledge in audiovisual production and design, as well as a strong interest in further developing my expertise in the audiovisual field. I combine both creative and analytical thinking, with exceptional attention to detail, resilience, and commitment to every project I take part in. My background in a technical high school specialized in computer science provided me with valuable skills such as programming and web design, as well as strong problem-solving abilities. Currently, I am pursuing one of the most essential areas of filmmaking—cinematography, lighting, and camera work—at Universidad del Cine (FUC).
